# Ethics and Safety Guidelines

The `rea-image` tool is designed for educational, analytical, and technical reverse engineering of visual assets. All users, developers, and AI agents interacting with this software must adhere to the following principles:

---

## 1. Respect for Intellectual Property & Likeness
- **Creative Study**: Use this tool to understand layout, lighting rigs, color theory, and photographic technique.
- **No Plagiarism**: Do not use reverse prompts or recreation blueprints to clone copyright-protected artistic works, brand trademarks, logos, or personal likenesses without explicit authorization.
- **Attribution**: Always respect the original creators and adhere to platform content guidelines.

---

## 2. Probabilistic Evidence, Not Definitive Accusation
- **Digital Forensics Limits**: Synthetic media detection is inherently probabilistic. High JPEG compression, format re-encoding, and screen recording can attenuate forensic signals or create false positives.
- **No Accusations**: Never present `rea-image` classifications as irrefutable proof that a specific person's photograph or content is fake.
- **Transparency**: Reports explicitly state what evidence would change or reverse the verdict (e.g. original camera RAW files, signed C2PA credentials).

---

## 3. Privacy & Biometrics
- **No Facial Identification**: `rea-image` does not perform facial recognition, identity matching, or personal biometric profiling.
- **Local Processing**: By default, `rea-image` operates completely offline. No image bytes or metadata are uploaded to third-party cloud servers unless the user explicitly configures an opt-in model endpoint.
